⚡ What is Mynah?

Mynah is a tool that turns spoken language into written text on your computer. It uses advanced technology to listen to audio files or microphones and writes down what people say. It works fast and does not need a constant internet connection. You can use it to help with note-taking, transcribing meetings, or translating languages in real-time.

This software runs locally on your machine. This means your voice data stays inside your computer and does not travel to a server. It provides high-quality results while keeping your information private.

💻 System Requirements

To run Mynah on your Windows computer, you need the following items:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
  • At least 8GB of RAM.
  • A modern processor (Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 or better).
  • A microphone if you want to record speech live.
  • Approximately 2GB of hard drive space.

📖 How to Use Mynah

After you open the app, you will see a simple control panel. To transcribe audio, drag and drop an audio file onto the window. The program will process the file and show the text on your screen.

If you prefer to use your microphone, select the "Live Record" button. The app will listen to your speech and show text as you speak. You can save these transcriptions as text files to your computer for later review.

Mynah recognizes many languages and works with different accents. It identifies words and provides time markers so you can see when each sentence occurs in the recording.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the app fails to start, verify that you have the latest updates for Windows. Sometimes, privacy settings in Windows prevent programs from using your microphone. Check your sound settings to ensure the permit list allows Mynah to access your audio input.

For better results, use a high-quality microphone. Background noise can affect the accuracy of the transcription. Choose a quiet space to record your speech for the best results.
